How it works

From your idea to installed

One team handles your job from start to finish, so the colours match, the sizes fit and nothing gets lost between suppliers.

  1. 01

    Request a quote

    Tell us what you need by WhatsApp, phone or the form below. Send photos and measurements of the wall, shopfront or vehicle, and let us know your deadline.

    • WhatsApp, call or form
    • Photos & sizes
    • Your deadline
  2. 02

    Design & approval

    Our in-house designers prepare your artwork and a mock-up showing how it will look on your vehicle, wall or product. Nothing goes to print until you approve it.

    • Artwork
    • Mock-up
    • Revisions
  3. 03

    Printing & production

    Everything is made in our workshop on Simu ya Upepo Road: vinyl, banner, fabric and DTF printing, plus laser cutting and sign building.

    • Large-format print
    • DTF printing
    • Laser & CNC cutting
  4. 04

    Installation & handover

    Our team fits signs, wraps and wall branding on site, or you collect finished items from the workshop.

    • On-site fitting
    • Workshop collection
